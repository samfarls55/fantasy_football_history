# Domain vocabulary

- **Person:** the manager whose career earned a result, distinct from a provider account or franchise.
- **Franchise:** a continuous league roster slot across ownership changes.
- **Season team:** a person's roster identity within one source league season.
- **Contest:** one regular game or playoff/placement/consolation pairing. A multiweek contest counts once for finalized W-L-T.
- **Performance / weekly leg:** a paired team's official weekly score and lineup. Completed legs remain eligible for weekly points and actual starts even when their contest is unfinished.
- **Bracket family:** main (`winners`) or lower (`losers`) provider endpoint. Match numbers are unique within that family, independently of weekly matchup IDs.
- **Advancement:** the source bracket's raw `w`/`l` progression; a lower-bracket advancement can belong to the lower scorer and differs from a scored win.
- **Championship path:** the main title contest and its winner-edge ancestors. Other main contests are placement; lower contests are consolation.
- **Game scope:** regular, championship playoffs, combined regular + championship playoffs, consolation, or placement.
