## Reading coverage and limits

Word files were read through their document XML, including table text, text boxes, available headers/footers, and notes/comments parts where present. PDFs were read page by page through their text layer. Markdown was read in full. Native chart labels and cached values were checked separately. The local OCR pass processed all 245 embedded image assets; the recognized text was read and all 54 no-text results were visually checked. Many assets were simply highlighter strokes. Equations and tiny screenshot labels are not a character-perfect transcription and were not used for style quotations or prose measurements.

Text counts below are raw whitespace counts, including titles, prompts, citations, repeated text-box representations, and other artifacts. OCR and chart text are additional to these counts. A large count does not imply a large amount of Sam-authored prose.

All original source paths are included for traceability. The external resources linked from the documents were not followed as additional writing samples. All locations and sensitive record categories explicitly excluded by the handoff remained outside the review.
