## Q35 revised

References: `.impeccable/mocks/season-overview-refined.png` and `.impeccable/mocks/season-overview-refined-phone.png`.

The revision preserves the four season tabs and Standings default. Compact year selection, champion and runner-up facts, a Season records link, and a closed Season format disclosure replace the oversized context blocks. A single comparison table shows manager, record, season scoring relative to the league, and wins versus expected. There is no appended player-highlight strip. On a phone, each manager uses a divided row with the record and two labeled comparison results.

Scoring vs league is the already defined season-dominance percentage, based on regular-season points per game. Wins vs expected displays actual wins minus all-play expected wins, with half credit for ties. Positive values mean more wins than expected; negative values mean fewer. This display direction does not change Schedule Victims, which ranks expected wins minus actual wins. No new metric or public calculation panel is introduced. The fixtures are synthetic.
