# Fantasy football history

Every Sleeper league's history on one page.

## Who it's for

People in a Sleeper fantasy football league who want to settle arguments and relive past seasons.

## How it works

1. Enter a Sleeper username.
2. Pick one of that user's leagues. A user with one league goes straight to it.
3. See the league's history: champions, records, careers and head-to-head streaks.

History follows each league's links to previous seasons back to its first Sleeper season.

## Now

- Any Sleeper league, imported on first visit and refreshed when more than 12 hours old.
- Managers appear by Sleeper username.
- Local development preview only. Not hosted.

## Not now

ESPN or Yahoo leagues, accounts, per-manager and per-season pages, live scoring, and hosting.

## Principles

1. Careers follow people, not roster slots. A replaced owner keeps their own career.
2. Official scores and settings win. Missing data stays missing; it never becomes zero.
3. Lead with the stories a league argues about, and keep every screen free of helper text.
